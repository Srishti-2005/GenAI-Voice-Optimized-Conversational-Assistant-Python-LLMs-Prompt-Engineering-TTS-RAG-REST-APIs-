MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT - SERVICE LEVEL ADDENDUM (SLA)
Document ID: LEGAL-SLA-2025
Applicable Tier: Platinum & Enterprise Tier Customers

1. SYSTEM UPTIME GUARANTEE
The Service Provider guarantees monthly platform uptime of 99.95% ("Uptime Commitment"), excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 72 hours in advance.

2. SERVICE CREDIT CALCULATION
If platform uptime falls below the commitment during any calendar month, the Customer shall be entitled to the following SLA credits:
- 99.0% to 99.94% Uptime: 10% credit of monthly billing fees.
- 95.0% to 98.99% Uptime: 25% credit of monthly billing fees.
- Below 95.0% Uptime: 50% credit of monthly billing fees and right to immediate contract termination without penalty.

3. INCIDENT RESPONSE TIERS & SEVERITY LEVELS
- Severity 1 (Critical Outage - Platform Inaccessible):
  * Initial Response Time: Under 15 minutes (24/7/365)
  * Status Update Interval: Every 30 minutes
  * Target Resolution Time: Under 4 hours
- Severity 2 (Major Degradation - Core RAG Retrieval Impaired):
  * Initial Response Time: Under 1 hour (24/7/365)
  * Status Update Interval: Every 2 hours
  * Target Resolution Time: Under 8 hours
- Severity 3 (Minor Defect - Administrative / Reporting UI Glitch):
  * Initial Response Time: Under 4 business hours
  * Target Resolution Time: Under 48 business hours

4. DATA PORTABILITY & TERMINATION
Upon written notice of contract expiration or termination, Service Provider shall export all vectorized embeddings and raw customer documents in standard JSON/Parquet format within 14 business days.
All customer tenant data on primary databases will be permanently purged within 30 days of verified handoff.
